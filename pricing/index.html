Gems & Pricing

Last updated: 28 July 2026

BallTrials is free to play, with no adverts. There is exactly one thing you can pay for: Gems. Everything you can buy in the game is bought with Gems, so this page is the whole of our pricing — there is nothing else hidden behind a menu.

What Gems buy — and what they never buy

Gems buy cosmetics (skins, trails, celebrations, frames), the Battle Pass, and balls up to Rare, which are variety and not strength.

They do not buy power. The strongest balls are unlocked by trophies, by playing, and no amount of money skips that. There are no loot boxes and no randomised purchases anywhere in the game: you always see exactly what you are getting before you pay.

If your child plays this

Buying is behind a parental gate, and accounts have a monthly spending limit switched on by default. If Gems were bought without your permission, we refund it — see the refund policy. That applies even if the Gems were already spent.

Who you pay

Payments are handled by Paddle.com Market Ltd, which is the merchant of record: they take the payment, send the receipt and appear on your statement. BallTrials is made and run by Jaime Valls Vigil, United Kingdom.

Gem packs open together with the public launch of the game. Prices on this page are the ones you will be charged.
